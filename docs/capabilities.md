# Current capabilities

This is the entry point for current support boundaries. Detailed contracts live
beside their implementations; dated audits and benchmark reports record results
at their stated revisions and are not a current list of missing features.

| Area | Current support and boundaries | Authoritative contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chemistry ownership | Connected molecules; systems, coordinates, and derived perception have separate owners; hierarchy views indexed once per published topology, so walks between molecules, chains, residues, atom sites, and dense atoms cost what they return | [Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) |
| Conjugation and resonance | RDKit 2026.03.3 conjugation in default perception; explicit connected-group preparation and bounded contributor enumeration with all five RDKit flags; source chemistry remains unchanged | [Guide](resonance.md), [conjugation](../crates/kekule/src/algorithms/conjugation.rs), [resonance](../crates/kekule/src/algorithms/resonance.rs) |
| Measurements | Cartesian distances, angles, and signed dihedrals; optional consecutive-bond validation; deterministic bond dihedrals use CIP priority and atom-ID ties with fixed references and optional values; periodic preprocessing is explicit | [Measurement contract](../crates/kekule/src/structure/measure.rs), [bond dihedrals](../crates/kekule/src/structure/measure/bond_dihedrals.rs) |
| Builder placement | On a `ModelEditor` draft, in the host's one publication: hydrogens completed at ideal positions from fixed or inferred counts (no perception needed) and collapsed back into counts, with per-atom reports, represented stereo following them, and a named error for an atom over its valence; ideal bond lengths by element pair and order; an atom's shape (linear to octahedral, lone pairs from valence electrons and charge, conjugated nitrogen planar) and free directions, staggered and anti against a single neighbor's substituents; single atoms attached in place of an explicit hydrogen or at the free position nearest a direction, or placed as a new molecule with its own chain and `UNL` residue; new atoms named by element and the next free number in their residue; cost follows the edited atoms, not the model; no conformer generation or relaxation | [Placement](../crates/kekule/src/structure/placement.rs) |

| Element reference data | Symbols, Cordero covalent radii, and dipole polarizabilities. Van der Waals radii come in four columns: Charry–Tkatchenko free-atom (the default) and reference, which fall back to each other; Bondi 1964 (54 elements: Bondi's 38, completed from Mantina); Mantina's consistent Bondi-scale main-group set (44 elements, Rowland–Taylor hydrogen); and Alvarez (93 elements). These three never fall back to another column | [Radius sources and coverage](../crates/kekule/src/molecule/element_reference.rs) |
| Hydrogens | Explicit graph atoms and implicit counts; fixed counts survive reperception; conversions preserve information and stereo, on molecules and, with coordinates, on model drafts | [Hydrogen semantics](hydrogen-semantics.md) |
| SMILES | Parsing, interpretation, plain/isomeric/canonical writing, and supported CX radical/group input and tetrahedral group output; unsupported content fails explicitly | [Public format API](../crates/kekule/src/lib.rs), [stereo support](stereo-support.md) |
| SMARTS | Recursive queries, Boolean atom/bond logic, connectivity/valence/ring predicates, tetrahedral and alkene stereo, enhanced tetrahedral groups, tagged and topology matching | [Dialect and explicit exclusions](../crates/kekule/src/query/dialect.md) |
| Stereo/CIP | Represented tetrahedral, double-bond and atropisomer stereo; bounded CIP assignment; explicit cleanup and candidate detection remain separate operations | [Stereo support](stereo-support.md), [CIP options](../crates/kekule/src/algorithms/cip/mod.rs) |
| MOL/SDF | Independent records, V2000/V3000 interpretation and writing; automatic version selection and explicit unsupported-content errors; ensembles as records whose data fields carry each sample's weight and state, its state's energy, and the temperature, read back explicitly | [MOL/SDF writer](../crates/kekule/src/io/molfile_write.rs) |
| mmCIF | Independent blocks, coherent residue altloc selection and overrides, compatible coordinate/conformer ensembles, hierarchy/source reports, and model/ensemble output that, as the PDB's own files do, states as `_struct_conn` only the bonds templates and polymer linkage do not imply (links between residues, metal coordination, bonds of residues without a bundled template), so a 100,000-atom export reads back in seconds; component-table bonds whose coordination flag the source leaves unstated take it from the component template, so mmCIF and PDB readings agree on metal coordination; chains that share a label, such as appended copies of one model, written as instances of their own (`A`, `A-2`, …) under their author chain names and read back as such; fixed implicit-hydrogen counts fail unless omitted | [Alternate locations](mmcif-alternate-locations.md), [interpretation](../crates/kekule/src/io/structure_interpret/mod.rs), [writer](../crates/kekule/src/io/mmcif_write.rs) |
| PDB | One entry as typed records with hybrid-36 numbers; one model, coordinate-model ensembles, and explicit conformations through the mmCIF interpretation pipeline; `TER`-delimited polymers and `LINK`, `SSBOND`, and `CONECT` bonds; writing of models, collection items, and ensembles as `MODEL`s, round-tripping through the reader: hybrid-36 numbers, `TER` after each polymer, and `CONECT` for every bond the reader cannot derive from the bundled dictionary; identifiers that do not fit their columns, and chemistry, sample state, or cells PDB cannot carry, fail explicitly, the latter unless omitted; fixed implicit-hydrogen counts have their own omit policy | [Public format API](../crates/kekule/src/lib.rs), [records](../crates/kekule/src/io/pdb_document.rs), [interpretation](../crates/kekule/src/io/structure_interpret/pdb.rs), [writer](../crates/kekule/src/io/pdb_write.rs) |
| XYZ | Frames as typed records; one frame as a model, or frames of the same atoms as an equally weighted ensemble of one state; connectivity inferred from Cordero covalent radii plus 0.45 Å, single bonds (dative between metal and non-metal), zero implicit hydrogens, no bond orders, charges or stereo; per-frame reports of inferred bonds, fallback radii, close contacts and omitted columns; extended XYZ columns rejected unless reported as omitted, comment kept verbatim; documents built from a host's elements and positions interpreted as parsed files; reading only | [Public format API](../crates/kekule/src/lib.rs), [interpretation](../crates/kekule/src/io/xyz_interpret.rs) |
| Native format | Exact, versioned binary persistence of molecules, topologies, models, ensembles, and in-memory trajectories; optional stored perception validated on load; streaming, bounded, fuzzed decoding; fresh layout identity on load | [Specification](native-format.md), [public API](../crates/kekule/src/lib.rs) |

| Alignment | Kabsch fits, RMSD as stored, and aligned RMSD of models, ensemble samples, and trajectory frames over selections of one layout or atom correspondences between independent topologies: explicit pairs, dense order of equal layouts, or equal hierarchy labels (chain, residue number, insertion code, atom name) in the author or the mmCIF label namespace, with the atoms of each side left unpaired and labels naming several atoms refused by name; symmetry-corrected fits within explicit search limits; no imaging of periodic coordinates | [Alignment](../crates/kekule/src/alignment.rs), [correspondence](../crates/kekule/src/alignment/correspondence.rs), [labels](../crates/kekule/src/alignment/correspondence/labels.rs), [symmetry](../crates/kekule/src/alignment/symmetry.rs) |

The PDB and mmCIF writers, and `kekule-traj`'s trajectory writers, return a
`WriteReport` naming every omission an `UnrepresentablePolicy::Omit` made,
with the entities it affects; the XYZ writer also names the content XYZ can
never carry.

The DSSP investigation and force-field scalability/periodicity extensions remain
deferred. Raw reference disagreements are not automatically defects: comparisons
must account for input selection and documented scientific conventions without
discarding asserted fields or relabeling errors as agreements.

For maintenance validation, see [CI](../.github/workflows/ci.yml) and
[fuzzing](../FUZZING.md). The [scientific benchmark](../benchmarks/README.md)
compares Kekule nightly with RDKit, gemmi, Biotite and mkdssp on curated datasets;
its [known differences](../benchmarks/known-differences.toml) record current
gaps and intended policies. It is never a release gate.

The retired OpenFF [validation report](https://github.com/choutkaj/kekule/blob/ead90ca94f66e6b4f3082e5db49d6ddc042cc32c/benchmarks/openff/VALIDATION.md) recorded
parity plots, numerical differences and CPU timings for the 110-molecule panel and
two-model NAGL checks at its revision. The crate contract defines current support.
